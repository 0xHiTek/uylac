Thousand Blades: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Thousand Blades is a game made by Uylac. This policy explains what the game does with your information. The short version is that it collects none.

Information we collect

None. Thousand Blades has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not collect, store or share any personal information. It does not use the internet while you play.

Game progress

Your progress (stars, War Merit, unlocked heroes and settings) is saved only on your device. If your device is backed up to iCloud or a computer, that backup may include your game progress, under Apple's own privacy terms. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

Children

The game collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
