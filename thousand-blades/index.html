Thousand Blades: Support

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email support@uylac.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Common questions

Where is my progress saved? On your iPhone. It is included in your iCloud or computer backup. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

How do I play? Drag anywhere on the screen to move. Skills attack on their own. Tap MUSOU when the gauge is full.

Does the game need the internet? No. It plays fully offline, with no ads.
